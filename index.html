About me

From site drawings to software

I didn't start out as a programmer. I trained as a civil engineer at Zagazig University, and like most engineers I spent a lot of time on work a computer could do: repetitive modelling, checking and documentation.

My first fix was a handful of Dynamo scripts for Civil 3D. They worked, so I learned C# to go further, joined the BIM Automation track at ITI, and shipped my first Revit add-in. Since then it's been add-ins, APS web apps, and now AI agents for BIM. Alongside that I work on computational design and the geometry algorithms underneath it.

What I care about is simple: tools engineers actually want to use, code the next developer can read, and shipping instead of polishing forever. I also share what I learn on my YouTube channel.
